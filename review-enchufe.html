First Impressions

Setup took less than 10 minutes on both TVs I tested it on — plug it into the HDMI port, power it from the included USB cable, and follow the on-screen steps to sign in with your Amazon account. The new Fire TV interface finds what you want to watch noticeably faster than older Fire TV Sticks, and app launches feel snappy instead of sluggish.

Picture quality in 4K with Dolby Vision looks genuinely great on a compatible TV, and Wi-Fi 6E support means less buffering even with several devices on the same network.

Verdict

For under $50, the Fire TV Stick 4K Max is the easiest way to turn any TV into a fast, modern smart TV. Unless you specifically need Google TV or Roku's interface, this is the streaming stick most people should buy.
