First Impressions

The 11" display and thinner body make the Scribe feel more like a real notebook than any previous Kindle. Writing with the included Premium Pen has a genuine paper-like texture, and there's zero lag between the pen tip and the line appearing on screen.

The AI notebook summarization is more useful than it sounds — after a long meeting, tapping "Summarize" turned two pages of messy handwritten notes into a clean bullet list in seconds.

Pros and Cons

Pros

Cons

Verdict

If you're tired of losing physical notebooks or want to finally digitize handwritten notes without giving up the pen-on-paper feel, the Kindle Scribe is worth the price.
