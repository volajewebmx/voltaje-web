First Impressions

The Series 10 is noticeably thinner and lighter on the wrist than the Series 9, and the larger wide-angle display makes text and notifications easier to read at a glance without raising your wrist all the way up.

Sleep apnea notifications and the updated Vitals app add real health value beyond step counting — this feels less like a fitness gadget and more like a genuine health tool.
